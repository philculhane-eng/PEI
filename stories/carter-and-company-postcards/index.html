Carter & Co.: an Island postcard album

This illustrated Carter & Co. catalogue builds on Harry Holman’s publisher lists at Straitpost. His research and illustrated checklists provide its foundation. Cards from the PEI Postcards collection extend the listing and link to their own records; reference illustrations from Harry’s catalogue carry individual credits.

Carter’s postcards offer a wonderfully varied journey around the Island: bright harbours and fox farms, little country roads, city streets and summer hotels. George Carter’s Charlottetown business grew from seeds and stationery into books and postcards; Harry has traced postcard advertising to 1903. The cards that followed came in many different guises. A familiar picture might return with a new colour treatment, border, caption or publisher’s partner.
