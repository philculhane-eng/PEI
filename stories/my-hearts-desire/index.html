PEI’s Greatest Postcards – #10 – My Heart’s Desire

I’ve seen this postcard once. It came up at auction, and by the time I had to decide whether to make the next bid it was already a very expensive postcard. I decided to let it go.

I was the underbidder. Sixty-five cents separated my bid from the winning one. That figure has stayed with me. I stopped bidding, and the card went to someone else. Whether another bid would have won it is a question I still turn over.

In the case of the Montague first-train card, I saw a version when I started collecting and waited about ten years before one finally came up for sale. You can now see it at number two, read the printer’s caption for yourself and compare it with the other Montague views. That’s one way a long search can end. I’m still waiting to find out how this one ends. The train that did turn up

I’ve never seen a second copy. If there’s one in someone’s album, I’d very much like a chance to buy it before a seller discovers just how much I want it. That’s why you get a postcard-sized question mark here. No title, no view and no tantalizing clue from the back.

So that’s my tenth pick. I’ve left room for it.
