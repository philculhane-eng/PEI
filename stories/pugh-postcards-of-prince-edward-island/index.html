The history behind the picture

Pugh: the Island in numbered views

This illustrated Pugh catalogue builds on Harry Holman’s publisher lists at Straitpost. His research and illustrated checklists provide its foundation. Cards from the PEI Postcards collection extend the listing and link to their own records; reference illustrations from Harry’s catalogue carry individual credits.

Toronto publisher Pugh put a wide variety of Island scenes into circulation, from Charlottetown streets to country bridges, fox ranches and harbour views. The numbered groups give these cards a pleasing order: neighbours in a sequence can belong to the same town, share a printing style, or carry us abruptly to another part of the Island. The galleries keep those numbers together.
