The history behind the picture

McCoy: Island scenes from a Moncton printing shop

The McCoy Printing Company of Moncton gave Island photographs several different wardrobes. Coins, flags and crests decorated its early souvenirs; the Acadian Series brought colour to the same landscape. Carter and Taylor sold McCoy cards under their own names, while McCoy’s imprint remained on the back. With thanks to Harry Holman’s research at Straitpost and the Smith and Angley handbook described by VintagePostcards.ca, these albums gather the examples in the collection.

There is plenty of daily life here: a horse and Island wagon at Souris, people crossing Fortune Bridge, the Northumberland under steam and houses reflected in the water. The numbered Acadian cards follow their printed sequence. Within the other albums, the pictures run alphabetically, with extra copies and small decorative variations tucked beneath each view.
