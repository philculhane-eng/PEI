J. D. Taylor: postcards from a Charlottetown bookshop

With thanks to Harry Holman’s illustrated catalogues at Straitpost, the starting point for these albums, expanded here with cards from the collection.

James D. Taylor’s Charlottetown business combined printing, binding, books and stationery. Harry Holman traces it from Queen Street to the Morris Building on Victoria Row, with postcards advertised by 1907. The cards take us through the capital and out to the Island’s shores and rivers. Decorated souvenirs, full-colour streets and quieter monochrome views all carried the bookshop’s name.
