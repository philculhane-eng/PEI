J. D. Taylor: postcards from a Charlottetown bookshop

With thanks to Harry Holman’s illustrated catalogues at Straitpost, the starting point for these albums, expanded here with cards from the collection.

James D. Taylor’s Charlottetown business combined printing, binding, books and stationery. Harry Holman traces its move from Queen Street to the Morris Building on Victoria Row. Taylor’s postcards offer an inviting mix: country scenes in neat black frames, patriotic souvenirs, colourful city streets and small boats making the winter crossing. Turn them over and the different printers and editions begin to fall into families.

Albums follow their earliest dated examples in this collection, with undated groups last. Numbered sets run in sequence; the other albums run alphabetically by title.
