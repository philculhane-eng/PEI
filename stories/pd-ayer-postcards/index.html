P. D. Ayer: Island souvenirs from Moncton

P. D. Ayer & Co. published Island scenes in Moncton, New Brunswick. A red flag or a little coloured crest could turn a quiet photograph into a souvenir; on other cards, the picture filled almost the whole front. This album ranges from the familiar buildings of Charlottetown to the wharves of Cardigan and Montague, a wreck at St. Peters Bay, and a train steaming into the new Montague station.

The cards preserve some welcome names behind the pictures. St. Andrew’s Church explicitly credits the Reverend E. Smith, whose Cardigan views lead into The View from the Minister’s Window. The first-train card has a story of its own. Ayer’s patriotic designs are described at VintagePostcards.ca; Harry Holman also contributed an Island example to the Toronto Postcard Club’s discussion of Private Mailing Card backs.
