Rumsey & Co.: from city gardens to Island harbours

With thanks to Harry Holman’s illustrated catalogues at Straitpost, the starting point for these albums, expanded here with cards from the collection.

Rumsey & Co. was a Toronto stationery and postcard firm whose Island output ranges from colourful views of Charlottetown to photographic postcards of Alberton and the western countryside. Look closely at the cards and a whole small world opens up: boats waiting at the wharf, Main Street traffic, a lighthouse across the water, and gardens bright enough to invite a stroll.
