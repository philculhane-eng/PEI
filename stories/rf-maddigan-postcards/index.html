R. F. Maddigan: postcards from the grocer’s shop

This illustrated R. F. Maddigan catalogue builds on Harry Holman’s publisher lists at Straitpost. His research and illustrated checklists provide its foundation. Cards from the PEI Postcards collection extend the listing and link to their own records; reference illustrations from Harry’s catalogue carry individual credits.

Richard F. Maddigan’s name belongs to the everyday business of Charlottetown as well as its postcard history. He took over W. Grant’s Queen Street grocery in 1900, added a soft-drink business in 1906, and by 1911 was advertising eighty varieties of postcards. His cards bring together city buildings, quiet fishing places, farms and the vessels that served the Island. Different printers and formats gave this local imprint a remarkably varied appearance.
