PECO’s Toronto Years: Other Issues

A ferry comes towards us across the water. A lodge waits beside the road. A little map offers routes to the national parks. Around the edges of PECO’s larger numbered runs are these smaller invitations to travel.

These cards share the late bilingual Toronto back with the numbered PECO and IBR albums. Here are the remaining issues: unnumbered pictures and cards carrying the BG, C, HVC and LN prefixes. Their different subjects make an appealing little album together.

Small groups and individual souvenirs

Several of the remaining cards take us aboard or alongside the Abegweit. An approaching view, a broadside view and the companionway to the lounge offer different ways of remembering the crossing. Shining Waters Lodge, Lovers’ Lane and the national-parks route map carry the journey inland.

The lettered numbers are worth keeping intact. BG1 accompanies Beechwood Gardens, HVC1 identifies the Harbour View Cabins and Cottages card, and C1 appears on the Provincial Sanatorium. The two LN cards take us to Kensington: Victoria Street at LN1 and the Presbyterian church at LN9. These are useful threads for future additions.

For now, the eleven entries are arranged alphabetically. Each retains its printed code or its unnumbered label, and the links beneath repeated views lead to the other copies in the collection.

About this album

These cards belong to the back-design family of postcard 2314: the two-line bilingual heading, horizontal rule, Toronto company imprint and PECO maple-leaf emblem. Blue and black printing belong to this family. The collection’s earlier Ottawa backs and other Toronto layouts have their own guide.

The former 98-entry Toronto album is now divided between three pages: 23 IBR entries, 64 ordinary numbered PECO entries and 11 other issues. Together they retain all 158 cards from that album. Different pictures under one number remain separate; repeated photographs and small printing variations share an entry. Display titles regularise spelling and punctuation.
